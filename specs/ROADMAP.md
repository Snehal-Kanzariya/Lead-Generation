# Roadmap & Deferred Scope

**Deferred from MVP:** autonomous sending, discovery crawlers, proposals module & generator, conversations inbox, multi-user/roles, billing, predictive forecasting, command palette, Postgres migration.

## Business hypotheses (validate, don't assume)
- Start niche: salons/wellness and local service businesses in India with weak or missing websites; second niche: small professional services. Choose by reply data after the first 30–50 researched leads.
- Packages (prices are hypotheses): Digital Presence Starter (portfolio anchor ₹15–20k / $199–299), Inquiry & Conversion, Business Efficiency, Custom CRM & Automation.
- Calibrate score weights against closed deals after ≥ 20 outcomes.

## 90-day plan
1–15 foundation (this build) · 16–30 MVP loop in daily use · 31–45 compliant outreach experiments · 46–60 optimize + proposals · 61–90 SaaS validation with 3–5 tester agencies; decision doc. Not a guarantee of revenue or PMF.

## SaaS later
Tiers Solo/Growth/Team gated by research credits, AI usage, seats; prices only after cost analysis and willingness-to-pay interviews.
